## Step 0 — onset-semantics reconciliation (v2 prep evaluator vs v3 `bearing_witness.detect`)

(a) **v2 onset** (`prep/eval_harness.py::stage1_replay`, lines 57–90, reused unchanged by `prep/eval_harness_v2.py` line 83):
median/MAD are fitted on windows 1–10, but the scoring loop (`for _, row in df.iterrows()`) then tests **every** window —
windows 1–10 included — against those statistics with **two-sided** `|z| ≥ 5` in ≥ 2 of 4 feature groups, 3 consecutive, and
reports the first window of the run (`onset = i - PERSIST + 2`). So v2 **did classify baseline windows**: a window inside 1–10
can be scored against a median it helped form, and an onset below 11 is possible — which is exactly what produced
`Bearing3_5: onset_window = 9` in `prep/eval_results.json` / `prep/eval_results_v2.json` (windows 9, 10, 11 moved). v2's
`lead_time_min = files − onset` (line 88) inherits that.
(b) **v3 onset** (`bearing_witness/detect.py::ReplayDetector.push`, lines 91–109; `thresholds.py` v3): the first `baseline_n = 10`
pushed windows return `Stage1State.BASELINE` — not evaluated, `onset_window = None`; from window 11 on, **one-sided** `z ≥ +5`
(`one_sided = True`, `z_thresh = 5.0`) in ≥ `min_groups = 2` groups, `persist = 3` consecutive ABNORMAL windows, and
`onset_window = windows[-persist]` (first window of the confirming run). Earliest possible onset is therefore **11**; v3
structurally cannot reproduce "window 9".
(c) **Therefore**, of PREP_PLAN's carried v2 numbers: the per-bearing onsets are identical under both rules for 8 of 15 bearings
(B1_1 30, B1_3 59, B1_4 57, B1_5 32, B2_4 31, B3_1 19, B3_2 169, B3_3 342) — for these the v2 `files − onset` lead times are
comparable to v3 (same onset, same lead: e.g. B1_3 59 / 99 min in both `prep/eval_results_v2.json` and `eval/results_v3.json`);
7 bearings moved under one-sided z and are **recomputed from v3** (`eval/results_v3.json`), never quoted from v2: B1_2 32→37,
B2_1 100→453, B2_2 24→47, B2_3 26→128, B2_5 21→122, B3_4 52→1418, B3_5 9→11; and the v2 aggregate "lead 11–2519 min
(median 129)" is **dropped** from PREP_PLAN in favour of the v3 aggregate (files − onset over 15/15, v3 semantics stated:
**min 11 / median 99 / max 2519 min**, from `eval/results_v3.json` `summary.lead_min_median_max`; that median counts B3_5 at its
floor value 103 — over the 14 bearings without B3_5 the median is 98 min, min/max unchanged). B3_5's v2 lead (105 min from
onset 9) is invalid outright; its v3 value (103 min from onset 11) is the structural floor, not a measured onset — see the
Verdict below. All lead times above are `files − onset` in 1-min records; none is a RUL or prognosis claim.
